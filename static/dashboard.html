Documentation

Schedule Board

The Schedule Board is the central view for managing shifts. Every shift appears as a row in a table showing the work site, status, time window, required certifications, staffing progress, and action buttons.

Status badges:
  • Open — nobody assigned yet. Shows how many staff are needed.
  • 1/2 Staffed — some staff confirmed, more needed. Shows progress toward the minimum.
  • Filled — confirmed count has reached the minimum staff required.

Filtering and navigation

  • Date range — a From and To date picker filter shifts to a range. The default range is the next two weeks.
  • Site filter — narrow the board to a single work site, or show all sites.
  • Refresh — re-fetches shifts and updates the board.

Creating a shift

1

Click New Shift

Opens a modal where you select a work site, set start and end times, choose required certifications, and enter the minimum staff count.

2

Set required certifications

Toggle on any certifications the shift requires. Only staff who hold those certs will appear as candidates. You must add certifications in the Certs panel before they show up here.

3

Set minimum staff

The minimum staff number determines when the shift is considered "Filled." For a single-person shift, leave it at 1. For multi-staff shifts, set the number of people you need.

4

Save

The new shift row appears on the board with an "Open" status badge.

Assigning staff

Click a shift row (or the Candidates button) to open the candidate slide-over panel. Senzii ranks every available staff member using three scores:

  • Proximity — distance between the staff member's location and the work site (Haversine).
  • Skills — overlap between the staff member's certifications and the shift's required certifications.
  • Availability — whether the staff member's availability window covers the shift time.

The overall match score is a weighted combination of all three. Candidates are listed from highest to lowest score.

1

Select a candidate

Click a candidate card to select it. The selected name and score appear in the footer.

2

Click Assign & Confirm

The staff member is immediately assigned and confirmed. A success toast appears and the shift's confirmed count goes up.

3

Repeat for multi-staff shifts

If the shift needs more than one person, the slide-over stays open after each confirmation. The subtitle updates to show how many more are needed. The panel auto-closes once the shift is fully staffed.

Why no matches?

If no candidates are returned, the slide-over shows a diagnostic panel explaining why. Each reason is counted:

  • Not available during the shift time
  • Available but assigned to an overlapping shift
  • Available but too far from the work site
  • Missing one or more required certifications
  • No location on file

Click Why no matches? to reveal the breakdown.

Conflict badges

A candidate card may show a Conflict badge. This means the staff member is available but already assigned to an overlapping shift. You can still assign them, but the conflict is surfaced so you can decide.

Editing and deleting shifts

  • Edit — opens the shift modal pre-filled with the current values. (Currently saves as a new shift.)
  • Del — deletes the shift. Assigned staff are freed and the shift is removed from the board. This cannot be undone.

Where shifts come from

Shifts can be created two ways:

  • Manually, via the New Shift button on the board.
  • Automatically, by converting a staffing request from the Requests panel. When a client submits a request, an admin converts it into a shift with one click.

Assignments

The Assignments view lists every staff assignment across all shifts in your organization. Each row shows which staff member was assigned to which shift, the match score, the current status, who confirmed it, and the available actions.

Columns:
  • Shift — the work site name and shift start time.
  • Staff — the assigned staff member's name (or email if no name).
  • Score — the match score from 0.00 to 1.00, computed when the assignment was created.
  • Status — Pending, Confirmed, or Rejected.
  • Confirmed By — who confirmed the assignment (typically "admin").
  • Actions — Confirm, Reassign, or Unassign, depending on status.

Filtering

Three filters at the top narrow the list:

  • Date range — a From and To date picker filter assignments by shift start time. The default range is the next two weeks.
  • Staff — show only assignments for a specific staff member.
  • Status — show only Pending or only Confirmed assignments, or both.

Filters apply automatically when changed. Click Refresh to re-fetch the latest data.

Statuses

  • Pending — the staff member has been assigned to a shift but the assignment has not been confirmed yet. Pending assignments show a Confirm button.
  • Confirmed — the assignment is locked in. The staff member is committed to the shift. Confirmed assignments show a Confirmed ✓ badge and cannot be re-confirmed.
  • Rejected — the assignment was declined or removed. Rejected assignments remain visible for audit but cannot be acted on.

Confirming an assignment

1

Click Confirm

Opens a confirmation modal showing the staff member's name and the work site.

2

Click Confirm in the modal

The assignment status changes to Confirmed. The shift's confirmed count goes up on the Schedule Board.

Overlap protection: Senzii checks for conflicting confirmed assignments before confirming. If the staff member already has a confirmed assignment for an overlapping shift, the confirmation is blocked with a message describing the conflict.

Reassigning an assignment

Click Reassign to change which staff member is assigned to a shift — available on any assignment regardless of status.

1

Click Reassign

Opens a modal showing the current staff member and a dropdown of all other staff in your organization.

2

Select a new staff member

Choose from the dropdown. Only staff in your organization appear. The current staff member is excluded from the list.

3

Click Reassign in the modal

The assignment's staff member is updated. A new match score is computed for the replacement staff member. If the assignment was confirmed, the shift's assigned staff is updated as well.

Overlap check: The system checks the new staff member's existing assignments for time conflicts before completing the reassignment. If a conflict is found, the reassignment is blocked.

Unassigning an assignment

Click Unassign to remove a staff member from a shift entirely — available on any assignment regardless of status.

1

Click Unassign

Opens a confirmation modal showing the staff member's name and the work site.

2

Click Unassign in the modal

The assignment is deleted. If it was a confirmed assignment, the shift's assigned staff is cleared (or promoted to another confirmed assignment if one exists). The shift will need a replacement.

Where assignments come from

Assignments are created when you assign a staff member to a shift from the Schedule Board. Clicking a shift row opens the candidate slide-over, and clicking Assign & Confirm creates and immediately confirms the assignment in one step. The assignment then appears in this view.

Match score

The score is a weighted combination of three factors, computed at the time of assignment:

  • Proximity (30%) — Haversine distance between the staff member's location and the work site.
  • Skills (40%) — overlap between the staff member's certifications and the shift's required certifications.
  • Availability (30%) — whether the staff member's availability window covers the shift time.

Scores range from 0.00 to 1.00. When you reassign, a new score is computed for the replacement staff member using the same weights.

Staff

The Staff view lists every staff member in your organization. Each row shows contact details, certifications, availability windows, and the actions you can take on that person.

Columns:
  • Name — the staff member's display name.
  • Email — used for magic-link login and notifications.
  • Phone — optional contact number.
  • Address — used for proximity matching. Senzii geocodes the address to coordinates on save; you never enter lat/long directly.
  • Skills & Certifications — tags for every certification assigned to this staff member.
  • Availability — day and time-window tags showing when this person can work.
  • TZ — the staff member's IANA timezone (e.g. America/New_York).
  • Actions — per-row action buttons (see below).

Adding a staff member

1

Click Add Staff

Opens a modal where you enter the name, email, phone (optional), address, and timezone.

2

Set certifications

Toggle on any certifications this staff member holds. Only certifications you've already added in the Certs panel appear here. Certifications determine which shifts this person is a candidate for — a shift's required certs must overlap with the staff member's certs for them to show up in the candidate list.

3

Save

The staff member is created and appears in the table. A magic link can be sent immediately so they can set a password and access the staff portal.

Per-row actions

  • Send Link — generates a magic login link for this staff member. The link lets them set a password and access the staff portal without you creating a password for them.
  • Avail — opens the availability modal. Set one or more weekly availability windows (day of week + start/end time). Availability is used by the matching engine: a staff member only appears as a candidate for a shift that falls within one of their windows.
  • Add Cred — opens a modal to assign an additional certification to this staff member. You can set an optional expiration date. Only certifications that already exist at the org level are listed — if the list is empty, add certifications in the Certs panel first.
  • Del — archives the staff member. This is blocked if the staff member has any upcoming or in-progress confirmed assignments; remove or reassign those first.

How staff feed into matching

When you open the candidate slide-over on a shift, Senzii scores every staff member using three inputs from this view:

  • Address — distance to the work site (Haversine on geocoded coordinates).
  • Certifications — overlap with the shift's required certifications.
  • Availability — whether any weekly window covers the shift's time.

Staff with no address, no certifications, or no availability windows can still be created, but they will rank low or be excluded entirely from candidate lists. Fill in all three to get useful match scores.

Filtering

Use the Search name input in the top bar to filter the table by staff name. The list updates as you type.

Work Sites

The Work Sites view lists every location your organization staffs. Each row shows the site name, address, required skills, timezone, and the actions you can take on that site.

Columns:
  • Name — the site's display name.
  • Address — the street address. Senzii geocodes this to coordinates on save; you never enter lat/long directly. The coordinates are used for proximity matching against staff addresses.
  • Required Skills — tags for every certification a shift at this site requires. Staff without these certifications will not appear as candidates.
  • TZ — the site's IANA timezone (e.g. America/New_York). Shift times on the board are shown in this timezone.
  • Actions — per-row action buttons (see below).

Adding a work site

1

Click Add Site

Opens a modal where you enter the site name, address, and timezone.

2

Set required skills

Toggle on any certifications shifts at this site require. Only certifications you've already added in the Certs panel appear here. These become the default required certifications for every shift created at this site — you can still adjust them per shift when creating a shift on the board.

3

Save

The work site is created, geocoded, and appears in the table. It is now available in the site filter on the Schedule Board and in the New Shift modal.

Per-row actions

  • Edit — opens the site modal pre-filled with the current values. Change the name, address, timezone, or required skills and save. The update takes effect immediately on all new shifts created at this site.
  • Del — deletes the work site. This button is disabled when the site has shifts or staffing requests attached to it. The tooltip shows how many shifts and requests are blocking deletion — remove or reassign those first.

How work sites feed into matching

When you open the candidate slide-over on a shift, Senzii uses the work site in two ways:

  • Address — distance from each staff member's geocoded address to the site (Haversine). Closer staff rank higher on proximity.
  • Required skills — the site's required certifications flow into every shift created at that site. Staff must hold all required certifications to appear as candidates.

A work site with no address cannot compute proximity scores — staff will rank as if they are infinitely far away. Enter a real address to get useful match scores.

Filtering

Use the Search name or address input in the top bar to filter the table. The list matches against both the site name and address, and updates as you type.

Clients

The Clients view lists every client in your organization. Each row shows contact details, the company name, how many staffing requests they have submitted, and the actions you can take on that client.

Columns:
  • Name — the client's display name.
  • Email — used for magic-link login to the client portal.
  • Phone — optional contact number.
  • Company — the client's company name, if provided.
  • Requests — a badge showing how many staffing requests this client has submitted. No requests means the client has not submitted any requests yet; N requests shows the count.
  • Actions — per-row action buttons (see below).

Adding a client

1

Click Add Client

Opens a modal where you enter the name, email, phone (optional), and company name (optional).

2

Save

The client is created and appears in the table. A magic link can be sent immediately so they can set a password and access the client portal to submit staffing requests.

Per-row actions

  • Send Link — generates a magic login link for this client. The link lets them set a password and access the client portal without you creating a password for them. From the portal, clients can submit and track their own staffing requests.
  • Del — deletes the client. This button is disabled when the client has submitted staffing requests. The tooltip shows how many requests are blocking deletion — cancel or convert those requests first. A client with no requests can be deleted freely.

Clients and staffing requests

Clients are the source of staffing requests. When a client submits a request from their portal, it appears in the Staffing Requests view with a status of open. The Requests column on this table gives you a quick count of how many requests each client has submitted.

To act on a client's request, go to the Staffing Requests view, where you can convert open requests into shifts on the Schedule Board with one click.

Filtering

Use the Name and Company inputs in the top bar to filter the table. Both filters apply simultaneously — the list shows clients matching both. The list updates as you type.

Skills / Certifications

The Skills / Certifications view is your organization's master list of certifications. This is where you define the vocabulary that the rest of Senzii uses — every certification assigned to staff, required on shifts, or attached to work sites must first exist in this list.

Columns:
  • Name — the certification name (e.g. BLS, RN License, Forklift).
  • Added — the date the certification was created in your organization.
  • Actions — per-row edit and delete buttons.

Adding a certification

Type the certification name in the input at the top of the panel and click Add. The certification is created at the organization level and immediately available for assignment to staff, shifts, and work sites. Duplicate names are rejected with a toast.

Per-row actions

  • Edit — converts the name cell into an inline text input. Change the name and press Enter to save, or Esc to cancel. Renaming a certification updates it everywhere it is used — staff records, shift requirements, and work site defaults all reflect the new name.
  • Delete — removes the certification from your organization. This also removes it from any staff who have it assigned. You will be asked to confirm before deletion. There is no undo.

How certifications are used

Certifications defined here flow into three places:

  • Staff — when you add or edit a staff member, the cert picker shows every certification from this list. Staff must hold a shift's required certifications to appear as candidates.
  • Shifts — the New Shift modal cert picker is populated from this list. Required certifications on a shift filter the candidate list to only staff who hold them.
  • Work Sites — a work site's required skills become the default required certifications for every shift created at that site.

If the cert pickers in Staff, Shift, or Work Site modals are empty, it means no certifications have been added here yet. Add some in this panel first, then they will appear everywhere.

Tip: Use consistent, standardized names (e.g. Forklift not Fork Lift) to avoid confusion in candidate matching. Staff and shifts are matched by exact certification name.

Staffing Requests

The Staffing Requests view shows every request submitted by clients through the client portal. Each request appears as a card with the client's name and email, the requested work site, date, time window, minimum staff count, required skills, optional notes, and the current status.

Status badges:
  • Open — the request has not been acted on yet. Shows Convert to Shift and Cancel buttons.
  • Accepted — the request has been converted to a shift. The shift is now on the Schedule Board. Shows Cancel only.
  • Filled — the shift created from this request has reached its minimum staff count.
  • Cancelled — the request was cancelled. No further actions are available.

Acting on a request

1

Convert to Shift

Click Convert to Shift on an open request. Senzii creates a shift on the Schedule Board using the request's work site, date, time window, minimum staff count, and required skills. The request status changes to Accepted. The board and request list refresh automatically.

2

Assign staff

Switch to the Schedule Board, find the new shift row, and open the candidate slide-over to assign staff as you would for any manually created shift.

Cancelling a request

Click Cancel on an open or accepted request to cancel it. If the request was already converted to a shift, the linked shift is deleted and any assigned staff are freed. This cannot be undone.

Where requests come from

Requests are submitted by clients from the client portal. When a client signs in via magic link, they can fill out a request form specifying the work site, date, time window, minimum staff count, required skills, and notes. Each submitted request appears here with an Open status. A badge on the Requests nav item shows the count of open requests.

Filtering

  • Requester — filter by client name. The list updates as you type.
  • Work site — filter by work site name. The list updates as you type.
  • Status — filter by status (Open, Accepted, Filled, Cancelled, or All). Changing this filter re-fetches the request list from the API.

Billing

The Billing view shows your seat reservation and billing summary. Seats are the staff member slots you pay for on your subscription. This is where you add or remove seats and see how much you are being billed.

Seat Overview:
  • Reserved — the number of seats on your subscription.
  • In Use — the number of staff members currently in your organization.
  • Available — Reserved minus In Use. This is how many more staff you can add without purchasing more seats.
Billing:
  • Price / Staff / Mo — the per-seat price ($5.00).
  • Monthly Total — Reserved seats multiplied by the per-seat price.
  • Next Billing Date — when your subscription renews. If you are on a free trial, a badge shows the trial end date instead.
Subscription:
  • Status — your subscription's current state (Trial, Active, Past Due, Canceled).
  • Cancel Subscription — cancels your entire subscription and releases all seats. You will no longer be billed.

Adding staff members

1

Enter a quantity

Set the number of staff members you want to add in the Add staff members input.

2

Click Add Seats

Your subscription is updated immediately. You are billed a prorated amount for the added seats. The seat overview and Staff table both refresh.

Removing staff members

Set the number of seats to remove in the Remove staff members input and click Remove Seats. Your subscription is updated immediately with a prorated credit. You cannot remove more seats than you have available (Reserved minus In Use) — the input's maximum is capped automatically.

Canceling your subscription

Click Cancel Subscription in the Subscription section to cancel your entire subscription. All seats are released and you will no longer be billed. You can resubscribe at any time.

Reactivating your subscription

After canceling, a Reactivate button appears in the Subscription section. Click it to start a new subscription using your existing Stripe customer. If your original trial or billing period hasn't ended yet, you won't be billed until that date. If both have passed, billing starts immediately.

How seats relate to the Staff view

Every staff member you add in the Staff view takes one seat. When In Use equals Reserved, you have no available seats — you must add more seats here before you can add more staff in the Staff view.

If billing is not configured

If no Stripe subscription exists, the view shows a message explaining that staff member reservations will appear once a subscription is started. Contact your account manager to set up billing.

MCP Server

Senzii exposes a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server so AI assistants can read and manage your scheduling data directly — staff, shifts, assignments, work sites, clients, and more.

Endpoint: https://senzii.com/mcp
Transport: Streamable HTTP
Auth: OAuth 2.1 (automatic sign-in through Senzii)

How it works

When you connect an AI assistant to Senzii, it redirects you to the Senzii login page. After signing in, you approve the connection — and the assistant gets access to your scheduling tools. No API keys to copy or paste.

1

Connect your AI assistant

Open your AI assistant's connector settings and add the Senzii URL: https://senzii.com/mcp. See the Claude or ChatGPT tabs for step-by-step instructions.

2

Sign in and approve

You'll be redirected to Senzii to log in. After logging in, you'll see a consent screen listing what the assistant can access. Click Allow to approve.

3

Start chatting

Your AI assistant can now list staff, create shifts, manage assignments, and more. Ask it anything about your schedule!

Available Tools (18)

  • list_staff / get_staff / create_staff / delete_staff
  • list_clients / create_client
  • list_shifts / list_assignments / delete_shift
  • list_staffing_requests / convert_staffing_request / cancel_staffing_request
  • list_work_sites / create_work_site
  • create_staffing_request
  • list_certifications / create_certification / update_certification / delete_certification
  • add_staff_certification
  • send_staff_magic_link / send_client_magic_link
  • get_org_metrics
  • run_sql (admin only, read-only SELECT)

Permissions by role

The tools available depend on how you sign in:

  • Admin — full access to all tools (staff, shifts, clients, certifications, metrics, SQL)
  • Staff — manage your own profile, availability, certifications, and view/accept assignments
  • Client — manage your profile, submit and track staffing requests

Other Clients

Any AI assistant that supports the MCP Streamable HTTP transport with OAuth can connect to Senzii.

Connection Details

  • URL: https://senzii.com/mcp
  • Transport: Streamable HTTP
  • Auth: OAuth 2.1 with PKCE

OAuth Flow

The connection uses OAuth 2.1 — the same standard used by "Sign in with Google" buttons. The client discovers the auth endpoints automatically:

  • Discovery: https://senzii.com/.well-known/oauth-authorization-server
  • Registration: https://senzii.com/oauth/register
  • Authorize: https://senzii.com/oauth/authorize
  • Token: https://senzii.com/oauth/token

Most MCP-compatible clients handle this automatically — you just paste the URL and sign in.
