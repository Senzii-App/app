Refund Policy

Last updated: July 3, 2026

Summary: Senzii offers a 30-day free trial — no charge during the trial. After the trial, you are billed per staff member per month. You can cancel at any time. If you cancel during a billing period, you keep access through the end of that period and are not charged again. We do not issue partial refunds for mid-period cancellations, but will issue full refunds for billing errors or unauthorized charges.

1. Free Trial

Senzii offers a 30-day free trial for all new accounts. No credit card is required to start the trial. During the trial period, you have access to all features of the Service at no cost.

If you cancel during the free trial, you will never be billed. Your account and data will be retained for 30 days after cancellation, then permanently deleted.

2. Subscription Billing

After the 30-day free trial, your subscription begins and you are billed monthly. Billing is handled by Stripe. The charge appears on your card statement as "SENZII" or "STRIPE*SENZII".

Billing periods start on the date of your first payment and end on the same day number of the following month. For example, if your first payment is on July 15, your billing period runs July 15 – August 15, and the next charge occurs on August 15.

3. Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time from the dashboard or by contacting us at support@senzii.com.

  • During free trial: No charge. You will never be billed.
  • During a billing period: Your subscription remains active through the end of the current billing period. You are not charged again. No partial refund is issued for the remaining days in the period.
  • After cancellation: Your data is retained for 30 days, then permanently deleted. You can reactivate within that 30-day window and resume where you left off.

4. Refunds

4.1 Billing Errors

If you are charged in error — duplicate charge, incorrect amount, or a charge after you cancelled — contact us at support@senzii.com within 60 days of the charge. We will issue a full refund for the erroneous charge.

4.2 Unauthorized Charges

If you believe a charge was unauthorized, contact us immediately. We will investigate and issue a full refund if the charge was made without your authorization. You may also dispute the charge directly with your card issuer or bank.

4.3 Service Outages

If the Service is unavailable for more than 24 consecutive hours due to a failure on our end (not including scheduled maintenance or third-party outages), contact us and we will issue a prorated credit for the affected period.

4.4 No Partial Refunds for Mid-Period Cancellation

We do not issue partial refunds for cancelling partway through a billing period. Your subscription remains active until the end of the period you paid for. This is standard for SaaS subscription billing.

4.5 Non-Refundable Items

  • Charges for billing periods that have already completed (unless there was a billing error)
  • Charges for overage seats (additional staff members beyond your plan) that were used during the billing period
  • Charges from before you cancelled, if you cancelled mid-period and used the Service during that period

5. How Refunds Are Processed

Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method (the card used for the subscription). Refunds typically appear on your card statement within 5–10 business days, depending on your card issuer. We are not responsible for delays caused by your bank or card network.

6. Overages and Seat Changes

If you add staff members (seats) mid-period, you are charged a prorated amount for the remaining days in that billing period. If you remove staff members, the change takes effect at the start of the next billing period — no refund is issued for the current period since those seats were available to you.

7. Plan Changes

Senzii currently offers a single plan at $5 per staff member per month with all features included. If we introduce new pricing tiers in the future, this policy will be updated and existing subscribers will be notified in advance.

8. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, email us at support@senzii.com with:

  • Your account email address
  • The date and amount of the charge in question
  • The reason for your refund request

We will respond within 5 business days. Refund decisions are at our discretion, but we aim to be fair and reasonable.

9. Chargebacks

If you believe a charge is incorrect, please contact us first before initiating a chargeback with your card issuer. We can usually resolve billing issues faster than the chargeback process. Initiating a fraudulent or unwarranted chargeback may result in account suspension.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy at any time. We will notify active users by email of material changes. Your continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

For refund requests or billing questions, contact us at support@senzii.com.
